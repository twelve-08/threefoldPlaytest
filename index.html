Moves

Time runs upward; board 1 is the empty start, and the view zooms out as time is reached. The dashed board on top is the next move; use Expand to jump further ahead. Playing on a past board echoes the piece up through later boards; if a later board already holds that square, a new universe branches (or merges into a quantum piece, if enabled). Squares follow the board edges, as labeled on each board: letters run along the left edge (a1 is the left corner), numbers along the right edge (c1 is the front corner). You can\u2019t play on a universe\u2019s present (latest) board. # win, & tie. Flip notation: XFa3-A2 flips a piece, XFA2 flips a past board; a ~ after the square means it was placed flipped. Lines need matching orientation; quantum pieces count as both players and either orientation, and can't be flipped. A flip also flips that square on later boards in its universe. Soundproof stops past moves from echoing forward (quantum merges still echo). Tunneller adds a Wormhole mode: pick an existing piece, then the same square on a different board, to relocate it across universes or time.
